Support

CitizenIQ USA helps you study civics and practice for a U.S. citizenship interview.

Contact support

Email teslachangedmylife@gmail.com for help with the app, purchases, or privacy questions. Include your app version, iOS version and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment details, immigration documents, Social Security numbers or other sensitive identifiers.

Lifetime access and purchases

CitizenIQ USA offers a one-time in-app purchase for lifetime access to its study features. It is not an auto-renewing subscription. The App Store displays the applicable price before purchase.

To restore an existing purchase, open the app’s lifetime access screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used to purchase. If products are unavailable, check your connection and try again.

For billing and refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase support.

Interview practice

Tap Hear the Question to listen to a prompt. Practice your answer aloud, then reveal the answer or guidance and mark your own response. The app does not record your voice or automatically grade spoken answers.

Your study data

Study activity and preferences are stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details. No app account is required.

Official information

Practice results do not predict an official outcome. Verify current requirements and officeholders at USCIS before your interview.
