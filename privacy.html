Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy describes the current iOS version of CitizenIQ USA, provided by Castaldo Studio.

Information stored on your device

The app stores study progress, quiz results, studied question identifiers, selected test edition, language, state, optional interview date and related preferences locally on your device. These support progress tracking and your study plan. CitizenIQ USA does not provide an app account or transmit these study records to a Castaldo Studio server.

Spoken prompts

The app uses Apple’s speech synthesis framework to read text aloud. It does not request microphone access, record your voice, transcribe your responses, or automatically evaluate spoken answers.

Purchases

In-app purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks purchase entitlements to unlock access. Castaldo Studio does not receive your payment-card details. Apple processes purchase information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and sharing

The current app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, developer-operated tracking, or a developer-operated backend. Castaldo Studio does not sell your app study data. Apple may process diagnostics or store information according to your device settings and its policies.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to handle support and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Do not email immigration documents or sensitive identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until the app or its data is deleted. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data from that installation. Device backups may retain copies under your backup settings and may restore data later. Manage backups through Apple’s settings. Deleting local data does not erase Apple’s purchase history or cancel your purchase entitlement. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us.

External links and this website

The app links to official resources and these support pages. Those websites process visits under their own policies. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor technical information, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. This site has no added analytics, tracking scripts or contact form.

Children and changes

The app is a citizenship study tool and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through an app account. We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change.
